## ⚠️ Common SEO Mistakes to AVOID

### ❌ Don't Do:
1. **Keyword Stuffing** - Don't repeat keywords unnaturally
2. **Duplicate Content** - Each page must be unique
3. **Thin Content** - Minimum 800 words per page
4. **Ignore Mobile** - 60% of searches are mobile
5. **Slow Loading** - Aim for < 3 seconds load time
6. **Buy Backlinks** - Google will penalize you
7. **Copy Content** - Always write original content
8. **Ignore Meta Tags** - Every page needs unique meta

### ✅ Do Instead:
1. **Natural Keyword Use** - Write for humans first
2. **Unique Value** - Provide insights competitors don't
3. **Long-Form Content** - 1,500+ words for important pages
4. **Mobile-First** - Test on mobile devices
5. **Optimize Images** - Compress & use WebP format
6. **Earn Backlinks** - Create link-worthy content
7. **Original Content** - Share your expertise
8. **Optimize Every Page** - Don't skip any page
